Sorcery Brawl

by Eitan Nahari

Sorcerers

Gojo

Attack — Red: Tap for a quick shot, or hold to charge it into a bigger, stronger one. It homes in on the enemy as it flies.
Ability — Blue: A slow-moving orb that drags in anyone who touches it and holds them there until it fades.
Super — Purple: An unstoppable shot with no limit to its range that shatters any wall in its path.

Choso

Attack — Piercing Blood: Fires a steady stream of shots while held — but hold it too long and it overheats.
Ability — Flowing Scale: Toughens his skin for a few seconds, reducing incoming damage.
Super — Wave: A wide barrier that grows outward from him, pushing and trapping anyone caught inside it.

Naoya

Attack — Dash: A quick burst forward that strikes anyone caught in its path.
Ability — Freezeframe: Freezes anyone at point-blank range for a moment — hit them again while frozen to send them flying.
Super — Supersonic: A burst of speed that builds the longer he keeps moving, letting him smash through walls and slam into his opponent.

Sukuna

Attack — Dismantle: A fast slashing strike that lands at a slightly random angle.
Ability — Cleave: A heavy close-range strike that can be used several times in a row before it needs to recharge.
Super — Furnace: A slow-moving explosive that levels nearby walls and leaves anyone caught in the blast burning.

Megumi

Attack — Strike: A short-range hit for 20 damage.
Ability — Shikigami: Summons a shikigami that hunts down enemies. Divine Dogs deal damage up close, Nue deals damage and briefly stuns its target, and Rabbit Escape sends out a swarm of harmless rabbits. Only one of each shikigami type can be active for Megumi at a time, and recasting replaces the existing one.
Super — Mahoraga: Summons a pet with its own 200 health that hunts the enemy down and hits hard at point-blank range — but costs Megumi almost everything, leaving him at 1 health. The pet fights on his behalf as its own separate target — it doesn't shield him, so he's still one hit away from losing the round the whole time it's out. It does adapt to whatever's hitting it, taking less damage from the same attack each time it lands.
