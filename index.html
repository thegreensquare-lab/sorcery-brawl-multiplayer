Sorcery Brawl

by Eitan Nahari

Sorcerers

Gojo

Attack — Red: Tap for a quick shot, or hold to charge it into a bigger, stronger one. It homes in on the enemy as it flies.
Ability — Blue: A slow-moving orb that pulls in the nearest enemy — including enemy pets — and holds them there until it fades.
Super — Purple: An unstoppable shot with no limit to its range that shatters any wall in its path.

Choso

Attack — Piercing Blood: Fires a steady stream of shots while held — but hold it too long and it overheats.
Ability — Flowing Scale: Toughens his skin for a few seconds, reducing incoming damage.
Super — Wave: A wide barrier that grows outward from him, pushing and trapping anyone caught inside it.

Naoya

Attack — Dash: A quick burst forward that passes through enemy pets while damaging them, then can continue toward the enemy player.
Ability — Freezeframe: Freezes anyone at point-blank range for a moment — hit them again while frozen to send them flying.
Super — Supersonic: A burst of speed that builds the longer he keeps moving, letting him smash through walls and slam into his opponent.

Sukuna

Attack — Dismantle: A fast slashing strike that lands at a slightly random angle.
Ability — Cleave: A heavy close-range strike that can be used several times in a row before it needs to recharge.
Super — Furnace: A slow-moving explosive that levels nearby walls and leaves anyone caught in the blast burning.

Megumi

Attack — Strike: A quick close-range hit.
Ability — Shikigami: Summons a shikigami that hunts down enemies. Divine Dogs attack up close, Nue damages and briefly stuns its target, and Rabbit Escape sends out a swarm of harmless rabbits.
Super — Mahoraga: Summons a powerful pet that hunts enemies down and fights on Megumi’s behalf. It adapts to attacks, becoming more resistant to attacks it has already endured, but costs Megumi a huge amount of health.

Yuta

Attack — Slash: A quick close-range slash combo that can chain into a dash.
Ability — Copy: Sends out Rika to hunt an enemy. If she returns, Yuta copies their ability and can use it twice. If Rika is defeated, Yuta gains Cursed Speech instead.
Super — Love Beam: Summons Rika to fire a powerful stream of projectiles at enemies.

Hakari

Attack — Shutter Doors: Creates a large square attack area in front of him as two sliding doors close together and damage enemies caught inside.
Ability — Dance: Hakari stands his ground and sends out a powerful wave that pushes enemies and enemy pets away from him.
Super — Idle Death Gamble: Places a stationary Pachinko that heals Hakari over time while it remains standing.

Nobara

Attack — Nail Shot: Fires nails toward the enemy that damage anything they hit and leave an X marker where they stop.
Ability — Hairpin: Detonates every X marker on the ground at once, damaging enemies caught near the blasts.
Super — Resonance: Uses an Enemy Piece to strike its linked enemy, dealing heavy damage and stunning them.
T

Todo / Yuji

Todo Attack — Takada-chan: A short-range hit that deals 10 damage. If it misses, a semi-transparent Takada travels twice as far and deals half damage.
Yuji Ability — Black Flash: Dashes up to half the map. It passes through and instantly destroys bushes, stops when it hits a wall, and instantly destroys nearby breakable walls. Hitting an enemy deals 20 damage and gives Yuji 15-damage attacks for 3 seconds.
Super — Switch: Switches between Todo and Yuji.
